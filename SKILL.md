# Seasea Brand Skill (Global)

Author and revise Seasea-institute artifacts with the canonical brand system applied — positioning, voice, visual identity, bright-blue gradient discipline, and anti-default governance — so every output reads like a research-grade deliverable from the AI-native & FDE research institute inside the Musein (目音) main brand. Seasea is not a standalone external brand; outward-facing work carries the Musein master brand and routes to `musein-brand`.

## Strategic Frame

**Seasea is the AI-native & Forward-Deployed Engineering (FDE) research institute inside the Musein (目音) main brand.** It is the applied research and forward-deployed engineering layer that codifies expert judgment into ontology, permission graph, workflow DAGs, and execution journals — powering Musein's commercial creative canvas and the broader Seasea vertical stack — not a SaaS dashboard, not a chatbot, not a prompt library.

Seasea does **not** operate as a standalone outward-facing brand. Every artifact that reaches the public market must carry the **Musein (目音)** master brand; Seasea appears only as an institute-level identity — e.g. *"Seasea — AI-native & FDE Research Institute of Musein"* — on internal research decks, FDE engineering deliverables, and research/engineering recruiting and R&D communications that sit under the Musein umbrella.

The brand must communicate **earned intelligence, decision-grade rigor, and quiet authority** — never hype, never rainbow-AI playfulness, never consumer warmth.

**Ratified layer personality (2026-10-13)** — canonical source: `seasea-wiki/concepts/brand/group-brand-personality-seasea-modelapi.md` v1.0 (Roland Berger × Aaker; theory-derived, not user-research validated).

| Slot | Value |

| 主人格 | **胜任 Competence** — 严谨与现场验证，是它作为参谋的唯一资格来源 |

| 辅二 | **真诚 Sincerity（敢说「不做」）** — 证据不足时劝阻客户，坦承哪些结论待验证、哪些需求不值得产品化 |
| 品牌角色 | **参谋 + 架构师**（Strategist + Architect）—— 判断该进哪个现场、解决什么问题，再把判断落成部署蓝图 |

Archetype mapping: **The Sage** = 参谋的先见与判断；**The Ruler** = 架构师的蓝图与治理权威。二者服务于上表的人格结构，不另立第三人格。

**Public-visual rule (binding).** The Seasea bright cobalt-to-sky blue consulting register is **internal institute use only**. Whenever a Seasea artifact appears alongside or inside the Musein main brand in any public setting (investor material, enterprise RFP, event booth, press), it must be rendered in **Musein Dark-Cinematic** — Deep Black `#0A0A0A` surface · Off-White `#F5F4EF` text · Cinema Coral `#FF6B6B` sole accent — with Musein as hero and Seasea as footer / secondary. Never the blue system, never as an independent master brand.

### The Seasea Gradient (canonical)

```
Strategic Navy #1E3A8A  →  Electric Cobalt #0048FF  →  Lumen Sky #2BB8FF
        deep              decision-grade               insight-bright
```

Read this gradient as a **strategic narrative**: depth of judgment → decision moment → emergent insight. Never invert it. Never replace any stop with a non-blue color.

**Rules:**
- The Seasea gradient is the **only** decorative gradient. No magenta, no green, no orange in any gradient.
- Use the gradient sparingly: hero glow, headline underline, single CTA fill, single chart series, one accent line per layout. Never as the dominant page background.
- Insight Indigo + Boardroom White / Pure White are dominant; bright cobalt is the accent of intelligence — used like a highlighter, not like wallpaper.
- Gradient direction: 135° (top-left dark → bottom-right bright) is canonical; 90° horizontal is allowed; never radial-rainbow, never animated multi-hue.
- Functional colors (Verified Green / Audit Amber) are functional only — never decorative.

### Typography

| Level | Direction | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display / Editorial | **PP Editorial New** (italic for emphasis), Tiempos Headline, GT Sectra | Hero headlines, institute moments, big numbers in decks |
| UI / Body sans | **Geist** (preferred), Söhne, Neue Haas Grotesk | Product UI, sales decks, body copy |
| Data / Mono | **Geist Mono**, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono | Workflow node labels, code, data tables, audit metadata |

**Banned fonts** (per [`high-end-visual-design`](../high-end-visual-design/SKILL.md) defaults Seasea adopts): Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans, Helvetica, system-ui without override, Calibri, Times New Roman.

**Rules:**
- Editorial serif italic on the **first key noun** of a hero headline is a Seasea signature ("The *intelligence* operating partner"). Use sparingly — once per hero.
- Title : subtitle ratio ≥ 2.5×. No decorative letter-spacing on body. No centered all-caps body.
- Display serif used only for brand moments (hero, section opener, big stat). Not for nav, buttons, captions.
- Numbers in decks: tabular figures, Geist Mono or Geist with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Always tabular-aligned in tables.

### Layout

- 12-column grid, 1440px or 1920px reference; gutter ≥ 32px
- Whitespace ≥ 35% of any hero composition (premium = breathing room)
- One core insight per page / per slide / per fold
- Editorial alignment — left-aligned headlines, generous left margin (no centered hero text on landing pages)
- Footer source citation on data slides / data sections (Seasea is source-bound)
- Boardroom slide format 1920×1080, 5% top-left blank zone reserved (logo safe — never draw the logo inside generated images)
- `Privileged&Confidential` watermark in small light-gray bottom-right on every restricted slide

### Icons & Iconography

- **Phosphor Light**, **Remix Line**, or custom 1px line iconography only.
- Banned: Material Icons, FontAwesome solid, thick-stroked Lucide defaults, emoji, 3D glossy icons.
- Replace any "AI / sparkles / magic wand" icon with: a network node, a permission graph, an audit checkmark, an editorial dot, a layered card, or the Seasea gradient underline.

### Imagery

- Use abstract: ontology graphs, decision trees, layered card stacks, permission matrices, vector portrait silhouettes (line art, not photo), satellite ocean imagery (Seasea = sea + intelligence).
- Banned: stock-photo people in suits, handshake / coin / rocket / globe / lightbulb metaphors, AI brain illustrations, robot icons, chatbot speech bubbles, 3D character avatars, generative-art rainbow noise.
- If a person must appear, it is a **monochrome editorial portrait** (Insight Indigo duotone or pure black-and-white), never a smiling stock photo.

### Motion

- Eased cubic-bezier `(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (premium overshoot) or `(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` (calm). No `ease-in-out` defaults, no linear.
- Hover states: 200–280ms. Page transitions: 400–600ms. Hero reveal: ≤ 1200ms.
- Subtle parallax on Seasea gradient (max 12px translate) only on hero — never on body sections.
- Banned: bouncy springs, glitchy AI text reveals, neon flicker, rainbow shifts, infinite background animation that distracts from copy.

## GPT Image 2 Preamble (for Seasea-branded image generation)

Every Seasea GPT Image 2 prompt must open with this canonical preamble:

```text
Create a 16:9 executive strategy slide image, 1920x1080, for the Seasea Institute — the AI-native & Forward-Deployed Engineering (FDE) research institute inside the Musein (目音) main brand, positioned as the applied-research and forward-deployed engineering layer rather than a standalone external brand. Use a premium research-institute visual standard: editorial information hierarchy, generous whitespace, dense but scannable strategic content, crisp vector-like ontology diagrams, refined typography, and zero consumer-app playfulness. Background: Boardroom White / soft off-white. Primary text: Graphite Black on Insight Indigo for hero passages. Accent: bright Electric Cobalt to Lumen Sky gradient (only blue stops, never multi-hue), used only for active decision paths, key emphasis, and intelligence highlights. In the upper-left corner, reserve a blank square equal to 5% of the total image width — keep this area identical to the background, with no border, no placeholder, no icon, no logo, no framing. Do not draw any brand logo (Seasea or Musein). Add the exact text "Privileged&Confidential" in small light-gray type at the bottom-right corner.
```

After the preamble, every prompt must declare:

1. **Slide theme** — the page's strategic role and one-line hook
2. **Core content to show** — every factual element (named modules, numbers, ontology nodes) — never vague hype
3. **Visual direction** — composition, gradient placement, layout grammar, restated forbidden visuals (no stock-photo people, no rainbow, no robot/brain icon)

## Musein Main-Brand vs Seasea Institute Co-Lock

Seasea is **not** a standalone external brand. When a **Musein (目音) main-brand** artifact needs to surface the Seasea institute affiliation:

- The Musein (目音) wordmark owns the hero. The Seasea institute wordmark sits only in the **footer** or top-right secondary slot — never as the lead brand.
- Seasea's bright-blue gradient may **frame** the Musein artifact (top edge, footer rule) but does not override Musein's Cinema Register palette; the two identities stay visually distinct.
- Use the line: *"Powered by Seasea — AI-native & FDE Research Institute of Musein"* / *"A Musein (目音) company, with the Seasea Institute"* — never present Seasea as an independent master brand.

When a Seasea-institute artifact (internal research deck, FDE deliverable) references Musein product features or sibling sub-identities, label Musein as the main brand and Seasea as the institute.
